I am a postdoctoral researcher in the Structural Engineering group of the [LGCGM](https://www.insa-rennes.fr/en/lgcgm.html) laboratory at INSA Rennes, where I work on FACILA, a steel connection system for fixing timber-frame façades to reinforced concrete slabs. I received my PhD in Architectural Engineering from [Sejong University](https://en.sejong.ac.kr/eng/index.do), Seoul, in 2023, advised by [Prof. Kihak Lee](https://sejong.elsevierpure.com/en/persons/kihak-lee/).

My work combines full-scale testing with finite element analysis. Topics I have worked on:

- **Connections**: modular steel connectors (S-CN) for high-rise modular buildings, UHPC thermal breaks for concrete cladding, steel connectors for hybrid timber-concrete façades.
- **Seismic performance of non-structural components**: shaking table tests of lighting and wireway systems with pulley friction dampers.
- **Building acoustics**: impact sound of floating floors, sound insulation of thermal breaks, vibro-acoustic FE models.

I mostly model in ANSYS and LS-DYNA. LS-DYNA material cards and generators I use, such as CSCM for concrete, are collected on [LS-DYNA Material Hub](https://lsdynamat.github.io).
